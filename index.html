Research Interests

My long term goal is to develop a unified framework that learns and uses efficient representations of data, domain knowledge, and available observations to model complex systems. I am particularly interested in generative approaches to achieve this objective.

I work in the intersection of generative modeling and physical sciences. Scientific (inverse) problems are often ill-posed and non-unique, which motivates probabilistic sampling approaches. The strong priors offered by generative models can significantly enhance robustness and efficiency, while physics laws and principles, if incorporated properly, can further improve the quality and stability of generated solutions. Therefore, I am developing scalable, uncertainty-aware methods for generative models + scientific inverse problems.
